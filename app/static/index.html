About the Project

Developed by Amar Syakir Mazlan as a project for a Data Science course.

Stakeholder: Majlis Bandaraya Melaka Bersejarah (MBMB)

Objective: To research and build a bilingual machine learning system for real-time verification of local municipal rumors and misinformation.

Frequently Asked Qs

It uses TF-IDF word vectorization to extract structural language patterns (like exclamation frequencies, urgent keywords, and formal news phrasings) and matches them against a regularized Logistic Regression classifier trained on a 60,000+ article bilingual corpus.

The AI Confidence Score represents the probability calculated by the model. Higher percentages indicate stronger structural alignment with historically verified real announcements or known misinformation patterns.

Yes, machine learning models recognize linguistic patterns but do not possess real-time factual lookup. Always double-check critical municipal or national news using the official fact-checking links in the source section.
